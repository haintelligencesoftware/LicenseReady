Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026

Short version: LicenseReady does not collect, sell or share your personal information. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. Your study progress stays on your device.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or any identifier. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your selected profession, state and license type, your answers and practice-exam scores, bookmarks, your optional exam date and your Pro access status. This data is kept locally on your device. It is not sent to us. If you delete the app, this data is deleted. You can also clear it at any time with Profile → Reset progress.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. We only receive confirmation from Apple that a purchase was made so we can unlock Pro features. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for adults preparing for professional license exams and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and post the new version on this page.

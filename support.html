Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open any locked screen (for example Practice Exams and tap a locked exam) to open the Pro screen, then tap Restore purchases. You must be signed in to the same Apple ID used for the purchase.

Do the 3, 6 and 12 month packs renew automatically?

No. They end after the period and you will not be charged again. You can buy another pack or Lifetime at any time.

How do I change my profession, state or license type?

Go to Profile → My exam. Your questions update instantly and your progress is kept.

How do I reset my progress?

Profile → About → Reset progress. This clears answers, bookmarks and practice-exam scores, but not your purchase.

Are these real exam questions?

No. They are original practice questions written for study. They are based on public laws, codes and official exam outlines. They are not copies of real exam questions.

I found an error in a question.

Thank you. Email us the exam, the question text and what you believe is correct, ideally with a source (law section, code article or official outline).

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. All questions and practice exams are stored on your device.
